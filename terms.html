Terms of Service

Last updated 21 October 2026

These terms apply to every order with Qualitide, a motion design studio based in India that makes videos for clients worldwide. Each project also has a short written agreement; if the two differ, the project agreement wins.

1. What you get

The format, length and versions you order, as listed on the pricing section at the time you order: for a 30-second film, a 16:9 master, a 9:16 version and a 10-second ad cut. Anything else is quoted separately.

2. How an order works

  • You send a brief. We send a storyboard, normally within 12 hours. Your first storyboard is free, one per client.
  • If you approve it, we invoice 50%. Your production slot starts when that payment, the approval and your files have all arrived.
  • Delivery: 48 hours from the start of your slot for short videos and 30-second films; 72 hours for 60-second explainers; one extra day for a 4K master.
  • You receive a watermarked preview. When you approve the film, we invoice the remaining 50% and send the clean files once it is paid.

3. Revisions

Revisions are unlimited within the storyboard you approved, requested within 14 days of the preview, each turned around within 24 hours. A new concept, script or format counts as a new order.

4. Prices

Prices are in US dollars and are the prices shown on the site when you approve your storyboard.

5. Payment

Invoices are due within 7 days. If an invoice is more than 14 days late, work pauses until it is paid. Monthly Motion is billed at the start of each month, before work begins, and can be paused for a month instead of cancelled.

6. Refunds

At the storyboard stage you can walk away and owe nothing. Once you approve the storyboard and pay the first 50%, that payment is non-refundable, because production time is reserved for you. If we cannot deliver what was agreed, we refund what you paid for the undelivered work.

7. Ownership

When the final payment is made, you own the final film outright and may use it anywhere, forever, without credit. We keep ownership of our tools, code, templates and any unused concepts. We may show your film in our portfolio after your launch date, unless you tell us in writing not to.

8. Your materials

You confirm that you have the right to use everything you send us: logos, fonts, screenshots, product images, footage and music. Client films use music we license, or music you supply and license.

9. Confidentiality and AI tools

We keep your brief, files and unreleased work confidential and delete raw files 90 days after delivery. Our films are made with AI-assisted tools under human direction, and your files may be processed by those tools to make your film. We never use your material in another client's work.

10. Delays

Delivery dates move by any time we wait for your files, feedback or payments, and by days we are unavailable for reasons outside our control, which we will tell you about promptly.

11. Liability

Our total liability for any project is limited to the amount you paid for that project. You are responsible for the claims your video makes about your own product or service.

12. Disputes

If something goes wrong, email us first and we will try to fix it within 7 days. These terms are governed by the laws of India.
