The details

Inside every film
  1. Real motion blur. Rendered at 240 fps and blended to a 180° shutter, with directional blur on every fast move. No stutter, no ghosting. Each output frame averages four subframes, like a cinema camera shutter at 30 fps.
  2. Cut to the beat. Every click, swipe and title lands on the music's beat grid, and the big moment hits on the drop. We measure the track's beat grid, then place every event on it: a 128 BPM track gives a 0.469 s grid.
  3. Your real material. Your actual screens, products or listing details, rebuilt as live code, never guessed. Text stays razor sharp at any zoom, and numbers can count, roll and react. Never a stock laptop or a generic mockup.
  4. No templates. Every film starts from a blank canvas and has to beat our previous films with techniques we have never used. We keep a ledger of every effect we have shipped, and check each new film against it before production starts.
  5. Fast. Storyboard within 24 hours of your brief. Film 48 hours after you approve it (72 for a 60 s explainer). The clock starts when your storyboard is approved and your screens arrive. Changes don't restart it.
  6. Unlimited revisions. Anything inside the approved storyboard, for 14 days after the preview, with no fees and no counting rounds. Each change comes back within 24 hours. A brand-new concept is a new film.
  7. Sound designed. Licensed music plus custom sound on every click, cut and counter, mixed to −14 LUFS. The loudness YouTube, LinkedIn and X normalise to, so you're never quieter than the post above yours.
  8. Every format. 16:9 and 9:16, a 10-second ad cut with every 30 s film, full ownership, no watermark on final files. 1920×1080 masters, 1080×1920 verticals, H.264 MP4. 4K and ProRes on request.
How it works
  1. Send the brief. Your website or store link, what the video is for, the one thing viewers must get, and your brand kit. About ten minutes.
  2. Get your free storyboard. Within 24 hours: a scene-by-scene board with script and timing. Ask for changes, or stop there and pay nothing.
  3. Get the film. 48 hours after you approve: full HD in 16:9 and 9:16, the 10-second ad cut, music and sound. Revisions follow, inside the storyboard.

Hour by hour

  • Hour 0
    Brief received. Your brief, screens and files go into private storage only our studio can access. We read your product site and pull your brand colours and tone.
  • Hours 0–24
    Script, storyboard, style. A timed script, 6 to 8 storyboard frames and a design direction built around your brand, with any questions we need answered.
  • Approval
    You decide. The 48-hour clock starts once you approve the storyboard and send your screens. If the direction is wrong, you walk away owing nothing.
  • Hours 0–30
    Your product, rebuilt in motion. Your interface rebuilt as live code, then choreographed on the music's beat grid with a library of 447 motion components and 15 or more distinct effects per film.
  • Hours 30–40
    240 fps, blended to cinema. Rendered at 240 frames a second, blended four subframes per frame. Custom sound mixed under a licensed track, mastered to −14 LUFS.
  • Hours 40–46
    Quality check. Every film is checked against our written 12-point standard before you see it. Anything that fails goes back to the build.
  • Hour 48
    Preview, then every format. A watermarked preview first, then revisions as you need them. When you approve and the final 50% lands: the 16:9 master, the 9:16 vertical and the 10-second ad cut.

Our 12-point quality standard
  1. One continuous camera; cuts land mid-motion in a matched direction.
  2. The key moment lands on a beat.
  3. One signature idea, no filler.
  4. Every important message readable for at least one second.
  5. Contrast of at least 4.5:1 on key words.
  6. Every number on screen comes from one data sheet.
  7. One focal point at a time.
  8. No unintended dead frames.
  9. No text under 24 px at 1080p unless decorative.
  10. Real motion blur: no stutter, no ghosting.
  11. Your brand colours and type, followed exactly.
  12. Original to each film: its own look, motion and sound.

Launch films, product ads, App Store previews, fintech films, property reels, explainers, feature clips and social cuts. If your product needs to move, ask us.

Live-action footage, actors, or AI-generated people posing as customers. Everything we make is motion design.
