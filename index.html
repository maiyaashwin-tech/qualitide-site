Inside every Qualitide film

Built like cinema.
Delivered like software.

Eight things you get in every film, at every price. We don't ship without all eight.

240

Real motion blur

Rendered at 240 frames a second and blended like a cinema camera shutter. Fast moves smear smoothly instead of stuttering.

Each output frame averages four subframes, a 180° shutter at 30 fps. Very fast moves also get a directional blur pass.

1/30

Cut to the beat

Every click, cut and counter lands on the music, timed to the frame. The big moment hits on the drop.

We measure the track's beat grid, then place every event on it. A 128 BPM track gives a 0.469 s grid.

1:1

Your real material

Your actual screens, products or listing details, never a stock laptop or a generic mockup.

Your UI is rebuilt as live code, so text stays razor sharp at any zoom and numbers can count, roll and react.

0

Templates

Every film is one of a kind. Each one must beat our previous three and bring at least three techniques we have never used.

We keep a ledger of every effect we've shipped. New films are checked against it before production starts.

48h

Turbo delivery

Storyboard the same day you brief us. The finished film 48 hours after your production slot starts, 72 for a 60-second explainer. Launch dates don't wait, so neither do we.

The clock starts when your storyboard is approved and your screens arrive. Changes don't restart it.

∞

Unlimited revisions

We keep changing it until you love it. No change fees, no counting rounds.

Revisions cover everything in the approved storyboard. A brand-new concept is a new film.

−14

Sound, designed

The 12-point quality standard
01One continuous camera. Every cut lands mid-motion in a matched direction.
02The key moment lands exactly on a beat of the music.
03At least 12 distinct effects, something new every second or so.
04Every important message readable for at least a full second.
05Contrast of at least 4.5 to 1 on every word that matters.
06Every number on screen comes from one data sheet, so they always agree.
07No more than three effects stacked on any single moment.
08No dead frame longer than 0.15 seconds.
09No text smaller than 24 px at 1080p unless it's purely decorative.
10Real motion blur on every fast move. No stutter, no ghosting.
11Your brand's colours and type obeyed exactly.
12At least three techniques we have never used in any earlier film.

Questions founders ask

Straight answers.

What industries do you work with?

Today: software and AI, online brands, mobile apps, fintech and real estate, each with a sample film on this page. More industries are coming. If your product needs to move, ask us.

What do you need from us?

Whatever shows your world best. Software: screenshots or a Figma link. Brands: product photos and your logo. Apps: your app screens. Real estate: the listing photos and details. We tell you exactly what's missing in the storyboard, on day one.

What can't you make?

We don't film live action or hire actors, and we don't make AI people pretending to be real customers. Everything we make is motion design: type, graphics, your product, your footage, animated to the beat.

Why is the premium film only $600?

Because it's a limited offer, not our normal price. We've opened 11 premium seats at a discount, across every industry, and each one gets the full studio treatment: the same craft, the same turbo delivery, the same unlimited revisions. $600 is our lowest price yet. When the 11 seats are taken, a 30-second film goes to $990. And the risk is ours: you approve the storyboard before you pay anything.

What is a seat?

One seat is one client. Your seat price covers your first order, a Launch Pack counts as one seat, and Monthly Motion keeps the seat price for 12 months. When all 11 seats are taken, the standard prices shown on each card apply to new clients.

Can you add a voiceover?

Yes. Tell us in the brief and we quote it per project, with the voice options, before you approve the storyboard.

What does "turbo production pipeline" mean?

It's the production system we built to make studio-grade films fast: your storyboard the same day, and the finished film 48 hours after your production slot starts (72 hours for a 60-second explainer). Your slot starts once the storyboard is approved, the first 50% is paid and your files have arrived. Every order gets it, at no extra cost.

Are revisions really unlimited?

Yes. We keep refining the film until you love it, with no change fees and no counting rounds. Revisions cover everything in the storyboard you approved, requested within 14 days of delivery, each turned around within 24 hours. A completely new concept counts as a new film.

What's on the monthly plan?

Four short videos every month, whatever you need that month: a feature announcement, a 10-second ad, an onboarding clip, a social cut of your launch film. Each one matches your brand and your launch film, with 48-hour delivery and unlimited revisions. Your seat price is locked for 12 months, you can pause any month, and if you start within 30 days of a film, its price comes off your first month.

What exactly is the 10-second ad cut?

A short version of your film built for paid social: a hook in the first second, your product in motion, your logo and a call to action, in 16:9 and 9:16. It's what you put money behind on LinkedIn, X, Instagram or YouTube.

Is this made with AI?

Partly. The animation is built in code with AI-assisted tooling, which is why it takes 48 hours instead of weeks. The script, storyboard, timing and every design decision are reviewed by a person, and your interface is rebuilt exactly, never generated or guessed.

Will it match our brand?

Yes. Send your logo, colours, fonts and a Figma link or screenshots. The film uses your real screens and your real type. If you don't have a brand guide, the storyboard proposes a look for you to approve first.

What if we don't like it?

You see the storyboard before anything is animated. If the direction is wrong, say so and pay nothing. Once you approve it and pay the first 50%, that half is non-refundable, and revisions are unlimited within the storyboard.

Who owns the film?

You do, outright, once the final payment is made. Use it anywhere, forever, with no credit required. We show it in our portfolio only after your launch, and never if you ask us not to.

How does payment work?

Nothing up front. 50% when you approve the storyboard. You then see a watermarked preview; the other 50% is due when you approve the film, and the clean files go out the same day. Prices are in USD, by card or bank transfer, with invoices due in 7 days.
