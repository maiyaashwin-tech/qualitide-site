Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

This policy explains what Qualitide collects through this website and our projects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

  • What you type into the brief form: your name, work email, website or store link, industry, what you need, dates, your message, links and any files you attach.
  • Emails you send to team@qualitidestudio.com and the files in them.
  • One setting in your own browser: which page mode you chose (Qualitide or detailed). It never leaves your device.

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. If we add visitor statistics, we will use a cookie-free service and update this page.

Why we collect it

Only to reply to you, plan and make your video, invoice you and keep the records the law requires. We never sell your information or share it for marketing.

Where it is kept and who processes it

  • Briefs and project files are stored in our Google Workspace (Gmail, Google Drive) and in a private GitHub repository that only our studio can access.
  • AI tools, including Anthropic's Claude, process briefs and files to draft storyboards and build films.
  • Email goes through our email provider; payments through our payment provider, which has its own privacy policy.

If we emailed you first

We sometimes write to companies whose launches we think we can help with. We use only a work email address your company publishes or that is publicly listed for this purpose, together with public information about your product. We send at most three short emails. Reply "no" or email team@qualitidestudio.com and we will not contact you again, and we delete your details on request.

How long we keep it

Raw project files: 90 days after delivery. Briefs that do not become projects: up to 12 months. Invoices and payment records: as long as tax law requires.

Your choices

Email team@qualitidestudio.com to see, correct or delete what we hold about you, or to stop hearing from us. We reply within 7 days. Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU GDPR or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act give you these rights.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
